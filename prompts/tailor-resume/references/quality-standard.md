# Resume Tailoring Quality Standard

Use this checklist immediately before delivery.

## Truth and evidence

- Every candidate claim maps to the base resume or an explicit user-provided fact.
- No metric, tool, credential, responsibility, title, date, or result was inferred from the job description.
- Transferable skills are phrased at the level supported by the evidence.
- Unsupported requirements are absent from resume claims and appear in the gap report when useful.
- Every statement is something the candidate can explain honestly in an interview.

## Targeting

- The target title matches the posting and the candidate's evidenced level.
- High-priority supported phrases use the employer's wording where natural.
- Required qualifications receive more emphasis than preferred qualifications.
- Relevance improves through placement and proof, not repetition or keyword stuffing.
- The summary, skills, and strongest bullets reinforce the same target-role narrative.

## Summary

- Exactly four sentences and at most 75 words, unless the user requested another format.
- Sentence 1 explicitly includes the target title.
- Technical strengths, value, and soft skills are all supported.
- No first person, alternate job titles, empty superlatives, or redundant phrases.

## Skills

- Categories fit the target occupation.
- Every listed skill is evidenced or explicitly confirmed by the user.
- Exact ATS terms are retained when accurate.
- No artificial padding, duplicate variants, or implausible proficiency claims.

## Bullets

- Each bullet begins with a clear action and describes what was done plus method and outcome, output, scope, or purpose.
- Metrics are copied exactly from verified evidence.
- Bullets remain believable for the role, seniority, dates, and domain.
- Action verbs and sentence openings are varied naturally.
- Tense, punctuation, and length are consistent and readable.
- Keywords appear in context and no bullet becomes a tool list.

## Format and delivery

- The base resume's chronology, section order, and template are preserved unless a change was requested.
- Prefer a one-column ATS-safe layout only when creating or redesigning a format; do not silently replace a supplied template.
- Do not introduce tables, graphics, icons, headers, or footers when ATS parsing is a priority unless specifically requested.
- Spelling, capitalization, dates, and contact details are consistent.
- Any unresolved fact is visibly marked for confirmation.
- No unsupported numerical “ATS score” is reported.
